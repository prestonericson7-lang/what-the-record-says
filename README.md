# [Title to be set]

**What the government's own documents, the patent record and the sleep laboratories say about radio, phones and the brain, read document by document, and what is left out.**

Compiled with Claude (Anthropic's AI). Every document is named by its number, date and holder so a reader can open the same page and check every line. Nothing is cited from a summary of a document without saying so.

First pass: 19 November 2026, done in one day. Each page ends with a list of what was read whole, what was read in part, and what was not read; that last list is the next work.

## Where it stands

The dark is real and it's the calendar. Records open at twenty-five years, the Air Force's Phase II is a 2000 denial letter, and nothing can be claimed from what's still shut. What reaches into it is the two leaks: Snowden on Panorama, 5 October 2015, with GCHQ's four Smurfs, and Vault 7 from 7 March 2017, authenticated the hard way when Joshua Schulte was convicted in July 2022 and given forty years. Listen, locate, power on, hide it, and the same for a Samsung TV. That's the real "more than you realize." It's also the deepest anyone has seen inside the dark, from two agencies in two countries, and neither file has a page that reads a head.

## If you have ten minutes

1. **The Air Force paid to speak into heads by radio, reported that it worked, and locked the file.** A 1993 Small Business Innovation Research contract, "Communicating Via the Microwave Auditory Effect," then a $739,995 Phase II whose public abstract says "the feasibility of the concept has been established using both a low intensity laboratory system and a high power RF transmitter." A 2000 letter from Brooks Air Force Base denies every page as classified. In 2002 and 2003 the Air Force patented the modulation method. (`01`, documents k and m.)
2. **What that effect is, in the Army's own 1998 words:** clicks and buzzes made inside the head by thermoelastic expansion of brain tissue under pulsed radio, "immediate," with "no residual effect after cessation," and in one experiment "communication of the words from one to ten." It is sound in. The same document's one concept for acting on a brain, a seizure induced by hundred-thousand-volt-per-metre nanosecond pulses, carries its own verdict: "The effectiveness of this concept has not been demonstrated ... No experimental evidence is available." Nothing in the record takes anything out. (`01`, document l; `02`, S12.)
3. **A Soviet machine that put people to sleep with pulsed radio, light and sound was patented in the United States in 1973**, and one was tested on a veterans' ward in California in the early 1980s, known only from a newspaper clipping. (`01`, document e; `02`, S4.)
4. **The Soviets irradiated the American embassy in Moscow for more than twenty years, and "the purpose of this irradiation has never been understood."** That sentence is the CIA's, 10 February 1976. In 1967 the Secretary of State raised it with Gromyko and said he "was not suggesting that it was intended to harm our personnel"; Dobrynin replied that the Americans were doing the same to the Soviet mission in New York. The staff were briefed in 1976. The one health study found nothing. The officer who ran the monitoring from 1977 to 1980 wrote in 2022 that the level inside the offices was about a tenth of a microwatt per square centimetre, a ninth of what a home router gives at three feet, that the intelligence community concluded the purpose was jamming American interception, and that one agency pushed the "mind control experiment" reading to pressure the State Department. The Pentagon's own answer to the signal, Project Pandora, cost $4,615,000, irradiated monkeys at 250 to 2,500 times the real level, produced "ambiguous and controversial" results with "no comprehensive project report," proposed putting eight people under the synthetic signal in 1969, with a cover story for their doctors, a plan "never carried out" over consent, was ended after an outside review found "no evidence of a behavioral change ... within the limits of any reasonable scientific criteria," and had part of its classified record destroyed in 1973; the Senate's 1979 verdict was that keeping the staff in the dark for twenty-three years "is not defensible." (`01`, document c; `02`, S2.)
5. **The American exposure limit was a thousand times the Soviet one**, 10 milliwatts against 10 microwatts per square centimetre, in the same CIA memo. A 1977 newspaper column the Agency kept says the Pentagon paid for nearly all the research "and preferred it that way," and that the researchers who found harm lost their contracts or were laughed at. The limit has since been tightened about tenfold. (`02`, S1.)
6. **The Army correlated its remote viewers' scores with the sky's electromagnetic weather and called the results a valid cause-and-effect phenomenon.** Center Lane-3, 1984, SRI International for Army INSCOM: ELF fields, sunspots, flares and geomagnetic indices against scored sessions from 1979 to 1984. Its own numbers: 779 correlations run, 38 expected by chance, 56 found, no correction for the number of tests, the ELF data "not sufficient" for significance, and a blind test recommended that the record does not show was run. (`01`, document h; `02`, S5.)
7. **Twelve patents, 1976 to 2022, claim to read or alter a brain by radio. None has ever been shown working.** The 1976 one, "remotely monitoring and altering brain waves," is cited by the 2022 ones. The Patent Office examines novelty, not function. What is real inside the chain is radar that reads breathing, which ships in a bedside product. (`01`, documents f, i, q.)
8. **Radio at the head does change sleep, in the laboratory, and in the direction of fewer remembered dreams.** A German government study in 2020, sixty people, nine nights each, a transmitter worn on the head all night at 2 to 6 watts per kilogram: fewer arousals, faster into deep sleep, and in women faster into REM and fewer awakenings. The dose is a legal maximum for a phone at the ear, for a whole night. A phone a metre away delivers ten thousand to a hundred thousand times less. (`01`, section 2; `03`, section 2.)
9. **The two things that most reliably erase a remembered dream travel with the phone and have nothing to do with radio:** a lit screen before bed cuts and delays REM (Harvard, 2015), and an alarm at the end cuts recall (a review of 69 awakening studies). (`01`, section 2.)
10. **The one documented case of a government spreading a metal compound over its own cities** is the Army's 1957 to 1958 drift tests with zinc cadmium sulfide over St. Louis, Minneapolis and others, judged in a 1997 National Research Council report to be below city background except at one unpopulated site. The patent often cited as "graphene in the clouds" is a real Abu Dhabi university application for a cloud-seeding particle, flown over the UAE and, in 2021, Texas. (`01`, documents a and q.)
11. **What is left out, plainly:** the contents of the Phase II contract; the records of the sleep machine's test; the Army's results pages; everything after 2000, which the calendar keeps shut; and any study that has ever put a phone beside a bed and counted dreams. (`02`, section 2.)

**The conclusion of the whole file:** every demonstrated government capability in the record puts something into a person, sound or heat, and takes nothing out; every claim to take something out is a patent nobody has shown working; the strongest laboratory effects on sleep need an antenna on the skull; and the deepest leaks show listening and locating. The question that started this, whether a phone beside a bed changes what a person dreams, has never been measured. `03_THE_TEST.md` is how to measure it, in three weeks, in a bedroom, blinded, and the requests in `02` are how to open the files that are shut.

## How to check any line

- A CIA document page is `https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/` followed by the document number in lower case. The reading room's search box answers only in a browser; search exact phrases in quotes and the program names: PANDORA, BIZARRE, TUMS, "Moscow signal", CENTER LANE, STARGATE.
- A patent is `https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/` followed by the number without commas. The scans have no text layer; read them as images.
- A laboratory abstract is `https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=` followed by the PubMed ID `&rettype=abstract&retmode=text`, which answers where the PubMed site itself puts up a cookie wall.
- The 1972 DIA report's full text is on the Internet Archive under its CIA number; the 1976 DIA report and the 1998 Army addendum are quoted from full transcripts on third-party pages, named in `documents/INDEX.md`, because the scans could not be fetched directly.

## The method

- **Names and numbers, not topics.** Program names, contract numbers and document numbers find the files; topic searches find newspaper clippings.
- **Read whole, or say how much.** Every document is marked "read whole" or "read in part" with the part named; a document found and not opened is listed as such and nothing is claimed from it.
- **Nothing from summaries without saying so.** Where a secondary source carries a claim (press accounts of leaks, a review of a study not read), it is named as secondary.
- **Two readings, kept apart.** A working frame (read as if the record hid something) and a plain reading (what the documents say on their face), both on the page.
- **Every number with its source, and every gap named with its size.**
- **Where it ends: a measurement.** The record cannot settle the question that started it, so `03` is a test anyone can run.

## Glossary for new eyes

- **Microwave auditory effect, RF hearing, Frey effect**: hearing clicks or tones inside the head when the head is hit by short pulses of radio; the pulse heats tissue by a millionth of a degree, the tissue expands, and the pressure wave reaches the inner ear. Sound in, from a transmitter.
- **SAR, watts per kilogram**: specific absorption rate, how much radio power a kilogram of tissue absorbs. The American limit for a phone at the ear is 1.6 W/kg over one gram; the laboratory sleep studies used 2 to 6 W/kg at the head.
- **Milliwatts per square centimetre**: the strength of a radio field passing through an area. The old American occupational limit was 10, the old Soviet limit 0.01, today's public limit at mobile frequencies about 1.
- **Pulse modulation**: switching a radio carrier on and off at a low rate (14 Hz, 217 Hz) as mobile signals do; the sleep laboratories find it is the modulation, not the carrier, that moves the EEG.
- **REM, N3, spindles, arousals**: stages and features of sleep as scored from the EEG; REM is when most remembered dreams occur; spindles are brief 11 to 15 Hz bursts in non-REM sleep; an arousal is a brief waking.
- **ELF**: extremely low frequency, 3 to 300 Hz; the band of the brain's own rhythms and of the Earth's natural electromagnetic noise.
- **MDR, Mandatory Declassification Review**: a request any citizen can make under Executive Order 13526, section 3.5, for a specifically described classified record to be reviewed for release. **FOIA**: the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552.
- **CREST, RIFPUB, RIPPUB**: the CIA reading room's database and its release markings ("released in full, public" and "released in part, public").
- **SBIR**: Small Business Innovation Research, the federal program under which the Air Force contracts in `01` were let.
- **Commit-reveal, hash**: the tools of `03`: a fingerprint of a plan recorded before a test begins, so the plan cannot be changed afterwards.

## What this is not

- It is not a claim that any government reads minds by radio, and not a claim that none does. The record shows what it shows and stops where the calendar stops, and this repository says so.
- It is not medical advice. Anyone whose sleep is disordered should see a clinician; the test in `03` is a log, not a treatment.
- It is not finished. The lists of unread documents at the end of `01` are the next work, and corrections are kept beside the lines they correct, never written over them.
